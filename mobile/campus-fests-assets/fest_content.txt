Spandan:
Overview: The official annual intra-institute, inter-hostel socio-cultural festival of IIT Jodhpur, celebrating internal student creativity, artistic expression, and hostel camaraderie.
Key Events: Features highly competitive inter-hostel tournaments across multiple fields including dance battles, music and band performances, dramatics, fashion shows, fine arts, photography, stand-up comedy, and literary quizzes—all tied to unique annual themes like Back to School.

IGNUS:
Overview: The inter-college socio-cultural fest of IIT Jodhpur, celebrating creativity, intellectualism, and artistic talent.   
Key Events: Features vibrant cultural competitions, Nukkad Natak, stage shows, and star musical performances by renowned artists like Shilpa Rao, Javed Ali, and Pentagram.    

Varchas:
Overview: The annual sports festival of IIT Jodhpur, dedicated to promoting sportsmanship and athletic excellence.
Key Events: Hosts national-level competitions across various sports, including football, cricket, basketball, tennis, badminton, volleyball, and athletics.
Soch (Social Aspect): A special initiative dedicated to community welfare, debates, and raising awareness about important social issues.

Prometeo:
Overview: IIT Jodhpur’s National Technical and Entrepreneurial Festival, celebrating innovation, tech advancements, and future-forward thinking.   
Key Events: Features immersive technical workshops, expert guest lectures, and exciting competitions exploring the evolution of technology and entrepreneurship.
